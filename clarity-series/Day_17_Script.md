# Day 17 — Morning Routine Jo Stick Ho

**Topic:** Morning study routine jo stick ho  
**Brand:** DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks  
**Length:** ~2 minutes

---

[0:00–0:08] HOOK  
Bhai, alarm 5 baje baja… aur tu 6:40 pe phone scroll kar raha hai.  
Routine plan kiya tha. Reality ne plan ko mute kar diya.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM  
Suna hai na — “5 AM club join karo.”  
Pehle 3 din josh. Phir 4th din late. 7th din guilt.  
Problem motivation ki nahi hai. Problem yeh hai:  
routine itni badi hai ki dimaag subah hi haar jaata hai.  
Phone bedside. Heavy syllabus. “Abhi 10 minute aur.”  
Phir poora din lagta hai — main fail ho gaya.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME  
Sun. Tu lazy nahi hai.  
Tu over-designed routine chalane ki koshish kar raha hai.  
Jo stick hota hai, woh cinematic nahi hota. Woh chhota hota hai.

[0:38–1:48] STEPS  

Step 1 — Wake window fix karo, wake time nahi.  
Exact 5:00 ka obsession chhodo.  
Ek 30-minute window choose karo. Jaise 5:30 se 6:00.  
Us window ke andar uthna = win. Guilt nahi.

Step 2 — Pehle 10 minute phone-free.  
Alarm ke baad phone ko doosre kamre mein rakho.  
Pehle paani, 10 jump, ek line likho: aaj ka sirf EK chapter.  
Phone baad mein. Brain pehle.

Step 3 — Mini stack, gym stack nahi.  
Zyadatar log yahan galt karte hain — 12 cheezein ek saath.  
Bas teen: uthna + 10 min start + 40 min deep study.  
Itna hi. Baaki extra hai.

Step 4 — Same cue, same seat.  
Har subah wahi mug, wahi table, wahi copy.  
Brain ko signal do: yahan padhai hoti hai.  
Decision kam, habit zyada.

[1:48–2:10] KEY TIP + CTA  
Sabse powerful tip:  
kal ki pehli 10 minute aaj raat set karo.  
Book khuli, pen ready, phone door.  
Subah willpower mat maango. Setup maango.

Aaj comment karo: STACK.  
Aur save karo — kal 5 baje wala tu thank you bolega.
