# Day 02 — Reels Se Focus Wapas Lo

Topic: Phone/Reels addiction se focus kaise badhayein
Voice: Naksh | Brand: DrDPKlass — Clarity that Clicks

## Script (~2 min)

**[0:00–0:06] Hook**
Bhai, tu padhai ke liye phone uthata hai... aur 40 minute baad Reels pe kisi ke pasta banate dekh raha hota hai.

**[0:06–0:24] Problem**
Scene yeh hai. Yeh phone addiction nahi hai. Yeh dopamine ka shortcut hai. Har swipe dimaag ko bolta hai: abhi easy wala reward lo. Isliye 10 minute padhai bhi bhaari lagti hai. Tu lazy nahi hai. Tera brain train ho gaya hai tezi se boredom hatane ke liye.

**[0:24–0:32] Promise**
Aaj main 4 simple steps dunga. Last wala sabse powerful hai.

**[0:32–0:52] Step 1 — Distance**
Pehla. Phone ko room se nikaal. Silent nahi. Door. Bag mein, dusre room mein, ya lock box. Willpower se nahi, distance se jeeta jaata hai. Agar phone aankh ke saamne hai, focus already haar chuka hai.

**[0:52–1:12] Step 2 — 25-5**
Doosra. 25-5 rule. 25 minute sirf ek chapter. Timer lagao. 5 minute walk, paani, stretch. Reels nahi. Zyadatar log yahan galti karte hain — break pe phone khol dete hain, aur break 40 minute ka ho jaata hai.

**[1:12–1:32] Step 3 — Friction**
Teesra. Home screen saaf karo. Social apps folder ke andar, last page pe. Notification almost zero. Phone ko tool banao, entertainment machine nahi. Jo cheez 2 tap door hai, woh kam khinchegi.

**[1:32–1:48] Step 4 — Slow start**
Ab suno sabse important cheez. Pehle 10 minute ka boring start accept karo. Pehle 10 minute dimaag fight karega. Uske baad focus aata hai. Reels isliye jeet jaate hain kyunki unka start instant hai. Padhai ka start slow hai. Slow start jeetna hi asli game hai.

**[1:48–2:05] CTA**
Aaj sirf yeh karo: phone doosre room mein rakh ke 25 minute padho. Comment mein likho — 25 done. Bhai, focus lost nahi hua. Woh sirf trained hai. Ab dubara train karo. Clarity that Clicks. DrDPKlass.
