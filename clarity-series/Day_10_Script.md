# Day 10 — Burnout Symptoms Aur Recovery

**Topic:** Burnout symptoms aur recovery  
**Brand:** DrDPKlass — Clarity that Clicks

## Spoken Script

Bhai, padhai ho rahi hai, phir bhi dimaag thaka hua lagta hai? Yeh laziness nahi hai. Yeh burnout ho sakta hai.

Roz uthte ho tired. Focus nahi tikta. Chhoti si cheez pe gussa ya rona. Padhai dekh ke hi energy khatam. Weekend pe bhi rest nahi milta. Zyadatar students sochte hain main weak hoon. Nahi. System overload ho gaya hai.

Burnout ka matlab yeh nahi ki tu fail hai. Matlab battery zero hai, aur tu charger ke bina daud raha hai. Fix discipline se nahi, recovery se hota hai.

Pehla step: naam do. Likh: main thaka hua hoon, lazy nahi. Neend, mood, focus — teen signs tick karo.

Doosra: load kaat, willpower mat badha. Aaj ke list se ek extra task hata. Phone ki last 1 hour screen cut. Body ko 20 minute walk ya stretch de.

Teesra: mini-recovery window. Roz 30 minute no-output time. Na padhai, na reels, na comparison.

Sabse important: full stop nahi chahiye — chhota pause chahiye. Aaj sirf ek load kaato.

Abhi note mein likh: lazy nahi — recovering. Save kar. Kisi dost ko bhej. Clarity that Clicks.
