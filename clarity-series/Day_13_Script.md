# Day 13 — Akela Feel Ho Raha Hai? Yeh Normal Hai

**Topic:** Loneliness feel ho raha hai – kya karein  
**Brand:** DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks  
**Length:** ~2 minutes

---

[0:00–0:08] HOOK  
Room mein log hain. WhatsApp pe 80 chats hain.  
Phir bhi andar se lagta hai — koi nahi hai.  
Bhai, yeh weakness nahi hai. Yeh signal hai.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM  
College hostel, PG, ghar — kahin bhi ho sakta hai.  
Reels dekh ke lagta hai sabke dost hain, tera koi nahi.  
Tu message type karta hai, delete kar deta hai.  
Akela feel karna fail hona nahi hai.  
Akela rehna aur connected na hona — yeh alag cheezein hain.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME  
Suno. Loneliness ko “main boring hoon” mat bana.  
Isko treat karo jaise body ka thirst.  
Thirst shame nahi — paani maangta hai.  
Aaj paani nahi, connection chahiye. Chhota wala.

[0:38–1:35] STEPS  
Step 1 — Naam do.  
Likh: “Aaj main lonely feel kar raha hoon.”  
Jab naam milta hai, dimaag drama kam karta hai.

Step 2 — One real ping.  
Ek insaan. Ek line.  
“Yaar, aaj thoda off feel ho raha hai. Free ho?”  
Perfect message mat soch. Send kar.

Step 3 — Shared space, not perfect friend.  
Library, class, gym, canteen — same jagah 20 minute.  
Dosti pehle proximity se aati hai, vibe se nahi.

Step 4 — Body ko lonely mat chhod.  
10 minute walk, 8 glass water, 11 baje phone bahar.  
Akele dimaag raat ko sabse zyada jhoot bolta hai.

[1:35–2:05] STRONGEST TIP + CTA  
Sabse important: loneliness ko hide mat kar comments mein.  
Aaj sirf ek cheez kar — ek real ping, ya 20 minute shared space.  
Comment mein likh: “PING” — taaki tu promise lock kar le.  
Save kar. Share kar us dost ko jo silent hai.  
Clarity that Clicks. Main hoon — DrDPKlass.  
Tu akela feel kar sakta hai. Tu akela nahi hai.
