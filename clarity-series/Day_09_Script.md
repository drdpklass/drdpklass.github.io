# Day 09 — Sleep Nahi Aa Rahi (Practical Fix)

**Series:** Clarity Big Brother · DrDPKlass  
**Length:** ~1:32 spoken

## Script

[0:00–0:10] HOOK  
Raat ke ek baj gaye. Aankh band hai, dimaag on hai. Kal paper hai, interview hai, assignment hai — lekin neend nahi aa rahi. Bhai, tu akele nahi hai.

[0:10–0:22] PROBLEM  
Scene yeh hai. Din bhar phone, late caffeine, bed pe padhai, aur phir blame karte ho ki sleep cycle bigad gayi. Yeh weakness nahi hai. Yeh mixed signals hain jo dimaag ko mil rahe hain.

[0:22–0:30] REFRAME  
Zyadatar log yahan galt karte hain. Unhe lagta hai so jaunga force se. Force se neend nahi aati. Body ko permission chahiye.

[0:30–0:33] OVERVIEW  
Simple sa four step night reset.

[0:33–0:40] STEP 1  
Step one. Cut-off. Sone se pachas minute pehle phone doosre room mein. Blue light dimaag ko din samajhti hai.

[0:40–0:49] STEP 2  
Step two. Paper dump. Jo kal ka tension hai, aath lines mein likh do. Dimaag tab tak loop karta hai jab tak usko paper pe utaar nahi dete.

[0:49–1:00] STEP 3  
Step three. Body cool-down. Chaar seconds saans andar, chhe seconds bahar. Paanch baar. Pairon ko thanda feel karo. Neend dimaag se nahi, body se start hoti hai.

[1:00–1:09] STEP 4  
Step four. Same time. Aaj gyarah, kal ek, parson do — yeh lottery hai, routine nahi. Same wake-up time neend ko train karta hai.

[1:09–1:18] INSIGHT  
Sabse important cheez. Bed sirf soone ke liye. Padhai, reels, fight, planning — bed ke bahar. Bed ko sleep signal banao.

[1:18–1:32] CTA  
Aaj raat ek kaam. Phone bahar, aath lines dump, paanch lambi saans. Comment karo SLEEP agar yeh try karoge. Save karo — raat ko kaam aayega. Clarity that clicks. Main hoon tumhara big brother.
