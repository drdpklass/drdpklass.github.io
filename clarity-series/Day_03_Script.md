# Day 03 — Kal Wali Padhai Aaj Shuru Karo

Topic: Procrastination kaise rokhein (students edition)
Voice: Naksh | Brand: DrDPKlass — Clarity that Clicks

## Script (~2 min)

**[0:00–0:06] Hook**
Bhai, syllabus dekh ke tu sochta hai: kal se serious ho jaunga. Kal aata hai. Phir same line.

**[0:06–0:24] Problem**
Scene yeh hai. Tu lazy nahi hai. Tera dimaag discomfort se bhaag raha hai. Badi task dikhti hai, dimaag bolta hai: abhi mood nahi hai. Mood wait karte-karte din nikal jaata hai. Phir guilt. Phir aur delay. Yeh loop hai, character flaw nahi.

**[0:24–0:32] Promise**
Aaj 4 chhote steps. Last wala sabse important hai. Open loop rakho.

**[0:32–0:52] Step 1 — Shrink the start**
Pehla. Task ko chhota karo. Poora chapter nahi. Sirf pehla page. Ya pehle 2 sawaal. Ya timer pe 8 minute. Start itna chhota rakho ki dimaag mana na kar sake. Mountain nahi, pehla kadam.

**[0:52–1:12] Step 2 — Two-minute lie**
Doosra. Two-minute lie. Khud se bolo: main sirf 2 minute try karunga. Zyadatar log yahan galti karte hain — perfect plan banate hain, start nahi karte. 2 minute start ho gaya toh momentum aata hai. Finish later. Start abhi.

**[1:12–1:32] Step 3 — Time, not vibe**
Teesra. Mood ka wait mat karo. Clock ka wait karo. Roz same time, same desk, same subject ka pehla bite. Habit time pe chalti hai, inspiration pe nahi. Jab dimaag bole "kal", jawab do: "abhi 8 minute".

**[1:32–1:48] Step 4 — Shame off**
Ab suno sabse important cheez. Guilt hatao. Kal miss hua, toh aaj extra drama mat karo. Miss ko story mat banao. Seedha next small start. Shame delay badhata hai. Kind start delay todta hai.

**[1:48–2:08] CTA**
Aaj sirf yeh karo: jo subject kal pe pada hai, uska pehla page 8 minute. Comment mein likho — 8 done. Bhai, tu postpone nahi kar raha. Tu start ko bada bana raha hai. Start chhota karo. Clarity that Clicks. DrDPKlass.
