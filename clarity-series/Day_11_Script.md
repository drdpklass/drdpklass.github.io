# Day 11 — Fail Hone Se Dar Kyun Lagta Hai?

**Topic:** Fear of failure overcome kaise karein  
**Brand:** DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks  
**Length:** ~2 minutes

## SCRIPT

[0:00–0:08] HOOK  
Bhai, tu start nahi kar raha kyunki tu lazy hai?  
Nahi. Tu start nahi kar raha kyunki fail hone ka soch ke dimaag freeze ho jaata hai.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM  
Paper, interview, pitch, even ek simple message bhejna —  
pehle dimaag mein movie chalti hai: “Kya log bolenge? Kya ho gaya toh?”  
Phir tu safe option pakadta hai: delay, hide, over-prepare.  
Scene yeh hai — fear of failure actually failure nahi rokta.  
Woh try hi rok deta hai. Aur try ruk gayi, toh growth bhi ruk gayi.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME  
Suno. Failure koi character certificate nahi hai.  
Woh data hai. Proof hai ki tu field mein hai.  
Jo log kabhi fail nahi dikhte, woh aksar field mein hi nahi utarte.

[0:38–1:48] STEPS  
Teen simple moves.

Step 1 — Fear ko naam do.  
Likh: “Main fail hone se nahi, judgement se darta hoon.”  
Naam diya, dimaag thoda shant hota hai.

Step 2 — Failure ko chhota banao.  
Poora future mat socho.  
Sirf poochho: “Sabse bura realistic outcome kya hai — aur uske baad meri next action kya hogi?”  
Plan B ready ho, toh dar kam padta hai.

Step 3 — Safe-to-fail version start karo.  
Full exam nahi — 10 minute mock.  
Full application nahi — ek draft.  
Full speech nahi — ek line practice.  
Chhota risk, jaldi feedback.

Zyadatar log yahan galt karte hain —  
woh perfect moment ka wait karte hain.  
Woh moment nahi aata.

[1:48–2:10] KEY + CTA  
Sabse powerful tip:  
Success count mat karo. Attempts count karo.  
Aaj ek chhoti cheez fail-safe mode mein start karo.  
Comment mein likho: “Attempt > Perfect.”  
Save karo. Share karo us dost ke saath jo soch-soch ke ruk jaata hai.  
Main hoon tumhara Big Brother. Clarity that Clicks.  
DrDPKlass.
