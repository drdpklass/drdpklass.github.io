# Day 07 — Degree Hai, Job Nahi? Yeh 4 Shifts Kaam Aate Hain

**Topic:** Graduate hone ke baad job nahi mil rahi  
**Brand:** DrDPKlass · Clarity that Clicks  
**Length:** ~2 min spoken

## Script (spoken)

[0:00–0:08] HOOK  
Degree haath mein hai. Result aaya. Ghar wale poochhte hain — ab job?  
Aur tu silently sochta hai — bhai, apply toh kar raha hoon… reply nahi aa raha.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM  
Yeh phase lagta hai failure. WhatsApp pe cousin ka offer, Instagram pe dost ka joining.  
Tu sochta hai — meri degree weaker hai kya?  
Sach yeh hai: degree job guarantee nahi karti. Market noisy hai. Applications bhi noisy hain.  
Tu lazy nahi hai. System confusing hai.

[0:28–0:42] REFRAME  
Job hunt exam nahi hai. Yeh sales process hai.  
Tu product nahi hai — tu ek solution hai.  
Bas 4 simple shifts. Last wali tip sabse powerful hai.

[0:42–1:05] STEP 1  
Pehla shift — 300 companies pe random apply band.  
Teen roles choose karo. Un teen roles ke liye skills aur examples likho.  
Spray-and-pray se inbox nahi bharta. Target se reply aata hai.

[1:05–1:25] STEP 2  
Doosra — CGPA ke peeche mat chhupo.  
Ek chhota project, ek skill demo, ek intern story.  
Recruiter 8 second mein proof dhoondhta hai, percentage nahi.

[1:25–1:45] STEP 3  
Teesra — daily 5 real applications, aur ek human message.  
Kisi senior, HR, ya alumni ko 4 line ka note.  
Algorithm se nahi, insaan se job milti hai.

[1:45–2:02] STEP 4 + STRONGEST TIP  
Chautha aur sabse important — rejection log.  
Har “no” ke saath likho: role, reason, next tweak.  
Shame mat collect karo. Pattern collect karo.  
Rejection personal nahi hai. Data hai.

[2:02–2:18] CTA  
Aaj sirf itna: ek role choose karo, teen companies likho, pehla message bhejo.  
Comment mein likho — job hunt start.
