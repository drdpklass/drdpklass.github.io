# Day 04 — Exam Hall Mein Dil Tez? Anxiety Control Karo

**Series:** Clarity Big Brother · DrDPKlass  
**Topic:** Exam anxiety kaise control karein  
**Length:** ~2 minutes spoken

---

## HOOK [0:00–0:12]
Bhai, paper haath mein aate hi haath thanda, dil tez, dimaag blank. Yeh tu weak nahi hai. Yeh exam anxiety hai.

## PROBLEM [0:12–0:32]
Scene yeh hai. Raat bhar padha. Subah sawal padha, memory lock. Phir dimaag bolega, main fail ho jaunga. Yeh loop padhai ko nahi, tere nervous system ko khaata hai.

## REFRAME [0:32–0:52]
Suno. Anxiety ka matlab yeh nahi ki tu ready nahi. Matlab body ko lag raha hai danger hai. Paper danger nahi hai. Hum body ko signal denge: safe hai.

## STEPS [0:52–1:40]
Simple sa plan.

**1.** Paper se pehle ya mock se pehle, sixty second ka 4-7-8 breath. Chaar count inhale, saat hold, aath exhale. Do teen round. Heart rate girta hai, dimaag khulta hai.

**2.** Pehla sawal easy wala. Sirf do minute. Poora paper nahi. Pehla page jeet. Momentum anxiety todta hai.

**3.** Fear ko naam do. Fail ho jaunga ko copy pe likho. Phir ek line plan. Aaj sirf teen sawal complete. Vague fear shrink ho jaata hai.

**4.** Body first, brain second. Do minute walk, paani, baith. Phir padhai. Body calm, tabhi recall aata hai.

## KEY INSIGHT [1:40–1:52]
Sabse important cheez. Exam hall mein tu syllabus nahi, apna nervous system manage karta hai. Jo student breath plus first easy question karta hai, uska dimaag lock nahi hota.

## CTA [1:52–2:10]
Aaj hi ek mock ya ek chapter pe yeh try kar. Sixty second breath. Phir pehla easy sawal. Comment mein likh: safe hai. Main hoon, tera Clarity Big Brother. Clarity that clicks.
