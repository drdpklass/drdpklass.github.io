# Day 06 — Anxiety Attack Aane Pe Kya Karein
**DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks**

## Full Spoken Script (~2 min)

[0:00–0:08] HOOK
Bhai, seene pe patthar, saans atak rahi, haath kaamp rahe…
Yeh weakness nahi hai. Yeh anxiety attack hai. Aur iske liye ek first-aid hai.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM
Exam hall, hostel room, bus, interview line — kahin bhi aata hai.
Dil tez, dimaag chillata hai “kuch bigad gaya”.
Tu sochta hai main pagal ho raha hoon.
Nahi. Body alarm baj rahi hai. False alarm. Par uss minute mein logic sunti nahi.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME
Scene yeh hai: pehle body ko safe feel karwana hai.
Phir dimaag shant hota hai.
4 simple steps. Phone pe save kar lena.

[0:38–0:55] STEP 1
Step one — naam do.
Bol: “Yeh anxiety hai. Yeh dangerous nahi hai. Yeh 10–20 minute mein utar jaayegi.”
Naam dena control laata hai.

[0:55–1:18] STEP 2
Step two — 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
5 cheezein jo dikh rahi.
4 jo chhoo sakte ho.
3 awaazein.
2 smells.
1 taste.
Dimaag ko present mein kheench lo.

[1:18–1:38] STEP 3
Step three — saans.
Naak se 4 count inhale.
Roko 4.
Muh se 6 count exhale.
Lamba exhale alarm band karta hai.
Teen round kaafi hain.

[1:38–1:52] STEP 4
Step four — ek safe person ko message.
“Main theek ho jaunga. Bas saath reh.”
Akela feel karna attack ko bada banata hai.

[1:52–2:08] STRONGEST TIP + CTA
Sabse powerful tip: attack ke baad guilt mat karna.
Paani pio, thandi bottle seene pe, 10 minute walk.
Aaj yeh 4 steps notes mein save karo.
Comment mein likho: MAIN SAFE HOON.
