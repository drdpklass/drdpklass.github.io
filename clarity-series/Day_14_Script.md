“Chapter 4 numericals” — itna specific.  
“Padhai karunga” nahi. Vague goal dimaag ko escape deta hai.

[0:52–1:12] STEP 2  
Step 2: Phone ko room se nikaal.  
Silent mode kaam nahi karta.  
Door ke peeche, dusre room mein, ya locker mein.  
Jo cheez aankh ke saamne nahi, uspe haath kam jaata hai.

[1:12–1:32] STEP 3  
Step 3: 25-5 ka ladder.  
25 minute deep, 5 minute stretch — paani, walk, no screen.  
Chaar rounds = 2 ghante.  
Pehla 25 jeet liya na, next easy ho jaata hai.

[1:32–1:48] STEP 4  
Step 4: Distraction dump.  
Beech mein soch aayi — “message karna hai” — sticky note pe likh, wapas kaam.  
Brain ko promise do: break pe dekhunga. Woh shant ho jaata hai.

[1:48–2:05] KEY INSIGHT + CTA
