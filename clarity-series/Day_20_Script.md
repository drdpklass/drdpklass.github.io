# Day 20 — Rejection Handle Karna Seekho
**Topic:** Rejection handle karna seekhein
**Brand:** DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks

## Full Script (~2 min)

[0:00–0:08] HOOK
Bhai, form reject ho gaya. Interview mein “we’ll get back” aaya, wapas nahi aaye. Dil pe lagta hai na? Tu weak nahi hai — tu human hai.

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM
Scene yeh hai: ek no aata hai, dimaag 10 no bana deta hai. “Main kaam ka nahi.” “Sab aage nikal gaye.” Raat ko replay chalta hai. Subah uthke apply karne ka mann nahi. Rejection event nahi — identity ban jaati hai. Aur yahan zyadatar log ruk jaate hain.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME
Suno. Rejection tera end nahi. Yeh data hai. Company ne tera future nahi decide kiya — unhone sirf aaj ka fit check kiya. Ab 4 simple steps.

[0:38–0:55] STEP 1
Step 1 — 10 minute dump. Paper pe likh: kya hua, kya feel ho raha, kya next. Phone pe rant mat kar. Feel ko naam de, phir box band kar.

[0:55–1:12] STEP 2
Step 2 — fact vs story alag kar. Fact: “Ek jagah select nahi hua.” Story: “Main kabhi select nahi hounga.” Story ko challenge kar. Ek line: “Yeh ek data point hai, meri value nahi.”

[1:12–1:32] STEP 3
Step 3 — 24-hour rule. Pehle din sirf recover. Doosre din ek chhota action: naya apply, feedback maang, ya skill 30 minute. Rejection ke baad freeze mat. Motion wapas la.

[1:32–1:48] STEP 4 + KEY TIP
Step 4 — rejection scoreboard. Kitne apply, kitne no, kitne yes. No ko count kar jaise gym ke reps. Sabse powerful tip: jis din no aaye, usi din ek naya haan ke liye move.

[1:48–2:05] CTA
Aaj comment kar: NEXT. Jo reject hua, uska next step likh. Save this. Share kisi dost ko jisko recently no mila. Main hoon tumhara Big Brother. Clarity that Clicks. DrDPKlass.
