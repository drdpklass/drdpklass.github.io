# Day 12 — Negative Self-Talk Change Kaise Karein
**DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks**

---

**HOOK [0:00–0:08]**
Bhai, exam se pehle dimaag mein kya chalta hai?
“Main nahi kar paunga. Main average hoon. Main fail ho jaunga.”
Yeh thought nahi hai. Yeh daily poison hai.

**PROBLEM [0:08–0:28]**
Scene yeh hai — tu padhta bhi hai, try bhi karta hai.
Par andar wala voice har baar pehle bolta hai: “tu kaabil nahi.”
Phir confidence girta hai, start late hota hai, result bhi weak lagta hai.
Zyadatar log sochte hain yeh honesty hai. Nahi. Yeh habit hai.

**REFRAME [0:28–0:42]**
Suno carefully.
Negative self-talk facts nahi bolta. Woh sirf worst-case story sunata hai.
Agar story galat hai, to life bhi usi story ke hisaab se chhoti ho jaati hai.
Bad news: voice automatically nahi rukta.
Good news: usko train kiya ja sakta hai.

**STEP 1 [0:42–1:02]**
Pehla step — catch the line.
Jab dimaag bole “main nahi kar paunga”, turant ruk.
Us line ko paper pe likh.
Naam de: “yeh soch hai, yeh sach nahi.”
Jo cheez naam le leti hai, woh control mein aati hai.

**STEP 2 [1:02–1:22]**
Doosra — evidence check.
Pooch: kya mere paas proof hai, ya sirf darr hai?
Ek column: kya galat ho sakta hai.
Doosra column: kya maine pehle kiya hai.
Bhai, past effort bhi evidence hai. Sirf failure nahi.

**STEP 3 [1:22–1:40]**
Teesra — replace, delete mat kar.
“Main nahi kar paunga” ko seedha “main topper hoon” mat bana.
Realistic line: “Yeh mushkil hai, lekin main aaj 25 minute try karunga.”
Brain jhoot nahi manta. Small honest line manta hai.

**STEP 4 [1:40–1:55]**
Chautha — voice ko time do, throne mat do.
Roz 3 minute “worry window” rakh.
Uske bahar woh thought aaye, bolo: “tu 9 baje aana.”
Dimag ko schedule milta hai, panic kam hota hai.

**CTA [1:55–2:10]**
Sabse powerful tip last mein:
Jo line aaj subah dimaag ne kahi, usko aaj hi paper pe flip kar.
Ek honest replacement likh, aur 10 minute action le.
Comment kar: “VOICE TRAIN.”
Save kar. Kal bhi yahi voice aayegi — tab ready rehna.
Clarity that Clicks. Main hoon DrDPKlass — tera Big Brother.
