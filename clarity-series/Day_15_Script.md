# Day 15 — Career Confusion After Graduation
**DrDPKlass | Clarity that Clicks**

## Full Spoken Script (~2 min)

[0:00–0:08] HOOK
Bhai, degree aa gayi. Photo bhi daal di. Par andar wala sawaal nahi gaya — “Ab main kya karun?”

[0:08–0:28] PROBLEM
Yeh confusion lazy hone ki nishani nahi hai. Yeh overload hai. Har taraf alag advice — MBA karo, govt exam do, coding seekho, business shuru karo. Tu sochta hai galat choice = poori zindagi kharab. Isliye tu decide nahi karta. Tu freeze ho jaata hai.

[0:28–0:38] REFRAME
Suno seedha. Career ek exam nahi hai jisme ek hi sahi answer hota hai. Pehla goal perfect path nahi — pehla honest experiment.

[0:38–0:55] STEP 1
Step one. Paper pe teen columns banao: Energy, Skill, Demand. Energy — kya karte hue time nahi pata chalta. Skill — abhi kya thoda-thoda aata hai. Demand — market mein kis cheez ki need hai. Jahan do columns milte hain, wahan se start.

[0:55–1:12] STEP 2
Step two. Ek “forever career” mat chuno. 90-din ka experiment chuno. Ek role, ek skill, ek proof. Example: 90 din content + analytics, ya 90 din GST basics + Excel, ya 90 din coding ke 20 mini projects.

[1:12–1:28] STEP 3
Step three. Advice filter lagaao. Har uncle ki baat mat lo. Sirf un logon se poochho jo last 3 saal mein woh kaam actually kar rahe hain. Baaki noise hai.

[1:28–1:45] STEP 4
Step four. Weekly scoreboard. Har hafte 3 cheezein: 1 skill hour daily, 1 proof piece, 1 human conversation. Job apply karna baad mein. Pehle signal banao ki tu serious hai.

[1:45–2:00] STRONGEST TIP + CTA
Sabse important: confusion tab tak rehta hai jab options dimaag mein ghumte hain. Jaise hi tu 90-din wala ek experiment lock karta hai, dimaag shaant hota hai. Aaj comment kar: LOCK. Apna 90-din experiment likh. Big Brother dekh raha hai. Clarity that Clicks.
