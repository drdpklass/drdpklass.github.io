        Privacy Note

        This ERP stores the details you enter (name, contact info, academic records, and any optional information you choose to add such as photo, Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details) in the college's private Google Sheet, accessible only to authorized ERP administrators of this college.

        • Optional fields (Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, demographic details, etc.) are never required to use the ERP — you decide what to share.
        • Your data is used only for academic administration, ID cards/certificates, attendance, assessments, and related college processes.
        • You can review or update most of your details any time from "My Account" or "Edit My Details," and can request correction or removal by contacting the college office.
        • Data is not sold or shared with third parties outside the college's administrative use.

        Questions about your data? Contact the college office or the ERP administrator listed in the footer.
