Most early-career Indian scholars do not fail because they lack ideas. They fail because every idea sits in the same state. A research pipeline is three manuscripts in different states: R-and-R, submission draft, and design or data.

Keep a one-page board with slot, twelve-word claim, next action, and blocker. Update it weekly. Protect deep blocks in light teaching weeks. Treat coauthors as capacity with written contracts. Put licence and IRB facts on the same board. Sequence three outlets by conversation fit. After a desk reject, retarget in 48 hours.
