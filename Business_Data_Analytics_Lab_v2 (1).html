How to Use This Lab

This is a self-contained learning app — everything runs locally in your browser, including on mobile data or fully offline once the page has loaded once.

No computer lab? No problem. Every practical works with taps and sliders — there's no software to install and nothing to type unless you want to write your own observations.

Save this file to your phone (or ask your instructor to share it via WhatsApp/pen drive) and open it anytime in your phone's browser — Chrome, Safari, or any modern browser works.

Tap the book icon at the top any time for a one-page formula & concept revision sheet covering every practical.
